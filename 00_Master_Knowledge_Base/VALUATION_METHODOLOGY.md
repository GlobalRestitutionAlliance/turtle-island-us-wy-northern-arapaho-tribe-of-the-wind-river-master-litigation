# Evidence-Backed Valuation Methodology

**Status:** TEMPLATE - verification required. Not legal advice.  
**Policy:** No published dollar pool without a versioned, cited workbook. All amounts remain `TBD (valuation pending)` until evidence-backed and human-reviewed.

## 1) Governing rules
- **No fabrication**: no invented amounts, sources, dockets, or citations.
- **Evidence gate**: every quantified row must cite a local hashed source artifact in `03_Evidentiary_Exhibits/Immutable_Ledger/` and a `CITATION_INDEX` entry.
- **Review gate**: `PENDING_LEGAL_REVIEW` by default; no `VERIFIED` without local hashed artifact + human sign-off.
- **Bands, not point certainty**: report `low / base / high` with confidence and assumptions.
- **Traceability**: every published number must trace to workbook row(s), formula, and citation(s).

## 2) Vector-to-valuation pipeline
1. **Identify/expand vector** - 1805 vectors is the minimum baseline and a goal; expand with verified evidence as new claims/harm vectors are documented.
2. **Capture evidence** - public source capture with provenance metadata, SHA-256, custody status.
3. **Classify harm** - takings/expropriation, forced labor, personal injury/death, cultural loss, unjust enrichment, statutory damages, etc.
4. **Quantify** - baseline amount, currency/year, adjustment method, interest/multiplier, offsets, low/base/high, confidence.
5. **Review** - researcher/counsel review; upgrade status only with sign-off.
6. **Aggregate** - dashboards/manifests aggregate only eligible reviewed rows.

## 3) Damages components
- **Historical baseline**: contracts, cadastral records, certified claims, official ledgers, appraisals, contemporaneous accounts.
- **Adjustment**: CPI/deflator or accepted economic index; cite index/source.
- **Interest/multipliers**: prejudgment interest or statutory multipliers only where legally applicable; cite authority.
- **Unjust enrichment/disgorgement**: traceable benefits/profits where evidence supports.
- **Offsets**: subtract documented settlements/compensation/credits.
- **Uncertainty**: confidence rating and sensitivity range required.

## 4) Publication rule
- Public-facing pool totals may be shown only as: `TBD (valuation pending)` or a reviewed range with methodology version, date, and citation coverage.
- Unreviewed/scaffold rows must never be summed into a public "confirmed" pool.
